## Deleting your account

You can ask for your account to be deleted at any time through a ticket in our [Discord server](/discord). Deleting an account anonymises it: your name becomes `DeletedUser_` followed by your account number, and your profile, links, comments, friends and clan membership are removed. Your scores stay on beatmap leaderboards under that name, as on osu!. The [privacy policy](/doc/privacy) explains what we keep and why.
