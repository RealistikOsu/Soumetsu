# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data RealistikOsu collects, why, and what you can do about it. RealistikOsu is run by the RealistikOsu team, who are responsible for your data. Our servers are in the European Union, and we follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## What we collect

**When you register and play:**

- **Account details:** your username (and any past usernames), email address and password. We only ever store your password as a one-way hash, never the password itself.
- **Profile:** whatever you add to it: avatar, banner, userpage, "also known as" name, country, favourite mode, playstyle and comments.
- **Gameplay:** your scores, replays, play counts, stats, achievements, first places and when you were last online.
- **Linked accounts:** if you link Discord, Twitch or an official osu! account, we store its account ID. For Discord we look up your current Discord name through our own lookup service, which asks Discord for it, when your profile is shown.
- **Support tickets and reports** you send to staff.

**To protect the server:**

- **IP addresses:** when you log in, register or use the site.
- **Hardware identifiers:** the game client sends hashed identifiers of your computer (such as hashes of your network adapter and disk). We use them, together with IP addresses, to catch multiaccounting, account sharing and ban evasion.

**On your device:**

- A **cookie** that remembers your browser for six months, used to protect your account and to spot ban evasion. It can't be read by scripts on the page.
- A **cookie** for your chosen language.
- Your **login token** and a few preferences (like hiding failed scores) in your browser's local storage.

We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

**If you donate:** the payment provider handles your payment details. We receive confirmation of the donation, the amount and the account it's for, never your card or bank details.

## Why we use it

- **To run the service** (performance of our agreement with you, under the [Terms of Service](/doc/terms)): your account, logging in, saving scores, leaderboards, your profile and supporter perks.
- **To keep it fair and safe** (our legitimate interest in a cheat-free, abuse-free server): IP addresses, hardware identifiers, the browser cookie, anti-cheat checks and moderation.
- **To contact you** about your account: verification, password resets and important changes.
- **Because the law requires it**, for example keeping donation records.

## Who else sees it

Your profile, scores, username, country and anything you post publicly can be seen by anyone. Email addresses, IP addresses, hardware identifiers and passwords are never public; only staff who moderate the server can see the first three.

We use these services, which process some data for us:

- **Cloudflare:** sits in front of the site and sees visitors' IP addresses and requests.
- **hCaptcha:** checks that you're human when you register or reset your password.
- **PayPal, Stripe and FreeKassa:** handle donations.
- **Brevo:** sends account emails.
- **Discord, Twitch and osu!:** only if you link an account, to verify the link and show your name.

Some of these companies are outside the EU. Where they are, the transfer is covered by the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

We never sell your data.

## How long we keep it

- **Account, profile and gameplay data:** for as long as your account exists.
- **When you delete your account,** we anonymise it rather than wipe it: your name becomes `DeletedUser_` followed by your account number, and we delete your email, password, profile, past usernames, linked accounts, comments, friends and clan membership. Your scores stay on beatmap leaderboards under the anonymous name, as on osu!.
- **IP addresses and hardware identifiers** are kept, even after an account is deleted, for as long as we need them to stop multiaccounting and ban evasion.
- **Donation records** are kept as long as the law requires.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your data,
- correct data that's wrong,
- delete your account (as described above),
- restrict or object to how we use your data,
- send your data to you in a portable format.

To do any of these, open a ticket in our [Discord server](/discord). We'll reply within a month. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

## Children

You must be at least 13 to use RealistikOsu. If you're under the age where the law where you live lets you agree to this on your own, you need a parent or guardian's permission. If a parent believes their child has given us data without that permission, they can contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll announce it on our Discord or on the site.
